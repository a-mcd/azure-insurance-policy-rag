# Question

While moving some furniture, I accidentally cracked the fixed glass in my patio door. Would my insurance pay for the repair?

# Answer

Yes — your policy can cover repairing the fixed glass in the patio door if the break was accidental and meets the policy conditions. The accidental breakage sections cover fitted or fixed glass in doors and windows (Gold and Platinum include this cover; Admiral lists it as optional) [1]. Accidental breakage is defined as sudden, unexpected and visible breakage caused by a specific external force [7]. You’ll have to pay any applicable accidental-damage excess shown on your Home Policy Schedule and the claim could be refused for listed exclusions (for example damage while the home is unoccupied or damage caused by tenants, pets or certain pests) [8]. If you have Admiral level you must check whether you bought the optional cover for glass; Gold and Platinum include it automatically [1].
