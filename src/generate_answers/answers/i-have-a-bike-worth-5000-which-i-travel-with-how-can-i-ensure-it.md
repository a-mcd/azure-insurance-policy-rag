# Question

I have a bike worth £5000 which I travel with. How can I ensure it?

# Answer

You must list it as a specified bicycle on your policy if you want cover for a £5,000 bike to be insured away from home.

Explanation:
- The standard unspecified bicycle limit is up to the “unspecified cycle limit” (not shown here) and pedal cycles are generally covered up to £350 worldwide under Contents cover [1, 4, 6, 3]. [1]
- Bikes worth more than the unspecified bicycle limit are classed as specified bicycles and “you must list these bicycles in your latest Home Policy Schedule to be fully covered.” [7]
- For bikes over £350 you can add cover for bikes up to £10,000 per bike (up to a £20,000 total) by listing them as specified bicycles (policy levels Admiral, Gold and Platinum all reference this option) [5, 8, 9].
- Specified bicycles are only covered anywhere in the world if the ‘Cover away from your home’ option for that bicycle is set to “Yes” in the Bicycles table of your Home Policy Schedule; accessories are limited to up to 10% of the specified bicycle value [2].

Material requirements and limits you must note:
- You need to list the £5,000 bike in your Home Policy Schedule as a specified bicycle to obtain cover at that value [7, 5].
- You must ensure the specified bicycle’s “Cover away from your home” shows as “Yes” if you want to travel with it and have worldwide cover [2].
- Accessories are covered up to 10% of the specified bicycle value [2].

I cannot confirm from these excerpts what your current unspecified cycle limit or current Home Policy Schedule entries are, or whether any extra security conditions or endorsements apply to this specific bicycle; you would need to check your Home Policy Schedule for those details.
