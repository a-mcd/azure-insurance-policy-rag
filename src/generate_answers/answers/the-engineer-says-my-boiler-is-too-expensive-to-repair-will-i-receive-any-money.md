# Question

The engineer says my boiler is too expensive to repair. Will I receive any money towards replacing it?

# Answer

Yes — if you have Home Emergency Extra cover, the policy provides £250 towards a replacement boiler declared beyond economic repair. [1, 5] 

Explanation:
- The payment of £250 towards a new boiler when the heating system is “beyond economic repair” is only included under Home Emergency Extra cover (not the standard Home Emergency cover). [5]  
- “Beyond economic repair” is defined as repair costs being more than replacement costs. [5]  
- The policy also separately provides up to £50 for temporary heaters while waiting for parts, which does not affect the £250 replacement contribution. [9]
