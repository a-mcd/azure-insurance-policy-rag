# Question

I have a bike worth £200 which I travel with. How can I ensure it?

# Answer

You can insure it as an unspecified bicycle under your contents cover — this will cover any bicycle up to the unspecified cycle limit (including anywhere in the world) so a £200 bike is within the standard pedal‑cycle cover of up to £350 if you have Contents Cover [3, 2].

What you should do and important points:
- Ensure you have Contents Cover: the pedal‑cycle cover applies only if Contents Cover is purchased [8, 2].  
- The standard worldwide pedal‑cycle limit is up to £350, so your £200 bike falls within this amount [2, 6, 4].  
- Accessories are covered up to 10% of the unspecified bicycle limit (so accessories would be within that 10% limit) [3].  
- Theft when left unattended is excluded unless one of the listed security conditions is met (locked to an immovable object with a security device when left outside or in a communal area; kept in a locked and secure building only you/family can access; or kept inside a locked vehicle or locked to it using a cycle rack) [3].  
- If your bike were worth more than the unspecified limit it would need to be listed as a specified bicycle in your Home Policy Schedule and you’d need to select “Cover away from your home” for worldwide cover; some specified bikes may require extra security conditions [5, 1].  

If you want cover beyond the standard £350 limit (not needed for a £200 bike), the policies allow adding bikes over £350 up to specified higher amounts by endorsement — for example add bikes up to £10,000 per bike subject to the stated maximums on Gold/Platinum/Admiral options [7, 9, 10].
