# Question

If someone broke into my detached garage and stole my tools. Which levels of home insurance would cover them, and how much could I claim?

# Answer

Admiral: No cover for theft from garages and outbuildings. Gold: Provides cover up to £5,000. Platinum: Provides cover up to £7,500.

Explanation:
- The guide’s coverage table shows theft from garages and outbuildings is not included for Admiral but is included for Gold and Platinum [3].  
- The Gold policy excerpt states “Theft from Garages and Outbuildings: Up to £5,000” [1].  
- The Platinum policy excerpt states “Theft from Garages and Outbuildings: Up to £7,500” [2].  

Conditions and important limits:
- The garage/outbuildings cover applies to theft of your contents kept locked in your outbuildings on the land your home stands on; it excludes money, credit cards and high‑risk items, theft from greenhouses, and loss caused by you, your family or anyone invited into your home, or while your home is unoccupied (unless the policy text elsewhere provides otherwise) [3].  
- How a contents claim would be settled (for example replacement or payment up to the amount to repair/replace) is determined by the insurer’s settlement rules [8].
