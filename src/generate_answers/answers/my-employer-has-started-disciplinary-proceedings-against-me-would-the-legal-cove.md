# Question

My employer has started disciplinary proceedings against me. Would the legal cover help with the dispute, or is this excluded?

# Answer

Direct answer: The policy’s legal cover will not help with employer disciplinary proceedings while those internal procedures are still ongoing — that is excluded for employment disputes, but legal defence cover may still apply in some circumstances. [2, 1, 4]

Explanation:
- Employment disputes cover applies to disputes relating to your contract of employment, but a dispute is deemed to have occurred only once all your employer’s disciplinary hearings and internal grievance procedures have been completed, and a claim for a settlement agreement while you are still employed is excluded. Therefore disciplinary or internal grievance procedures while you remain employed are excluded from the “Employment disputes” insured incident. [3, 2, 1]
- Separately, the policy’s “Legal defence” insured incident covers costs to defend you where, for example, a formal investigation or disciplinary hearing is brought against you by a professional or regulatory body, or certain criminal or civil actions arise from your work as an employee (with listed exclusions such as assault, alcohol/illegal drugs, HMRC investigations and driving). If your situation involves a formal disciplinary hearing by a professional or regulatory body or the other listed legal defence events, that part of cover may be relevant even though the employment‑contract dispute cover is not available during internal employer procedures. [4, 5]

What materially affects cover:
- If the employer action is an internal disciplinary or grievance process while you are still employed, employment‑dispute cover is excluded until those procedures finish. [2, 1]
- If the proceeding is a formal investigation or disciplinary hearing by a professional or regulatory body, or a qualifying criminal/civil matter as described under Legal defence, the Legal defence section may provide cover subject to its exclusions and conditions. [4, 5]

If you want a definitive view for your exact case, you would need to establish whether the proceeding is (a) an internal employer disciplinary/grievance while you remain employed (excluded under Employment disputes) or (b) a formal disciplinary/investigation by a professional or regulatory body or another event falling under Legal defence (which may be covered, subject to exclusions). [2, 1, 4]
