# Question

I have garden furniture worth £2000 will any policies cover it being left in the garden?

# Answer

Yes — but only up to the garden contents limit for your policy level.

- Admiral: garden contents (patio furniture) is not shown in the excerpts with a specific limit, but Personal possessions and Specified items rules show garden items cover is Optional or not included; the excerpts do not provide a garden-contents monetary limit for Admiral, so we cannot establish cover for £2,000 under Admiral from the supplied documents. [6, 9]  
- Gold: garden contents (items you keep in your garden such as patio furniture) provides cover up to £1,000, so a £2,000 loss would exceed this limit. [2]  
- Platinum: garden contents (items you keep in your garden such as patio furniture) provides cover up to £2,500, which would cover the £2,000 garden furniture within that stated limit. [4]

Other important points from the excerpts:
- Garden plants cover is separate and has different limits (Admiral £1,000; Gold £3,000; Platinum £5,000) and does not apply to furniture. [5, 1, 3]  
- Specified items and Personal possessions cover away from home are optional or conditional and exclude items on the land your home stands on, so they do not change the garden contents outcome above. [7, 9]  
- Theft from garages and outbuildings has a stated Gold limit (up to £5,000) but the excerpts do not state whether your furniture was in a garage/outbuilding; that cover therefore cannot be applied to the £2,000 garden furniture claim without that fact. [8]

If you want a definitive answer for your policy, confirm which level (Admiral, Gold or Platinum) is on your Home Policy Schedule and whether the furniture was left in the garden or stored in a locked garage/outbuilding.
