# Question

If my home became unsafe to live in after a fire, how much would each level of cover pay for somewhere else for my family and pets to stay?

# Answer

Each level of cover provides the following maximum for temporary accommodation for your family and pets if your home is unsafe to live in after a fire:

- Admiral: provides cover up to £50,000 for temporary accommodation for your family and pets following an insured event [5].  
- Gold: provides cover up to £75,000 for temporary accommodation for your family and pets following an insured event [4].  
- Platinum: provides cover up to £100,000 for temporary accommodation for your family and pets following an insured event [6].

Additionally, if you have Home Emergency cover or Home Emergency Extra cover (optional), this pays on a reimbursement basis up to £500 (HEC) or up to £1,000 (HEX) for alternative accommodation, with a maximum of £250 per night [9, 10].
