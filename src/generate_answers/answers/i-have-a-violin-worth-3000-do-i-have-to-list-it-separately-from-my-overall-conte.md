# Question

I have a violin worth £3000. Do I have to list it separately from my overall contents?

# Answer

Yes — you must list it as a specified item if it is worth more than £2,000. The policy defines musical instruments as high‑risk items and says any high‑risk item worth more than £2,000 must be specified and listed in your Home Policy Schedule [1, 8, 4]. If you do not list it, the insurer will not pay more than the ‘Unspecified item limit’ for it (and high‑risk items above that limit are excluded) [7, 4]. For Admiral, Gold and Platinum the specified‑item facility applies (with differing maximums) so you should add the violin as a specified item under the level that matches your policy [2, 3, 6].
