# Question

What policies cover my phone if I take it on holiday?

# Answer

Your phone is covered by Platinum personal possessions anywhere in the world; Gold and Admiral provide cover away from home only if you have chosen the optional personal possessions / specified items cover for “cover away from your home”. [2, 5, 4, 1]

Explanation
- Platinum level: personal possessions are covered anywhere in the world under the Platinum policy wording, so a phone counted as a personal possession is covered while on holiday (subject to the personal possessions limits and other exclusions) [5, 2].  
- Gold level: personal possessions (and specified items you have asked to cover away from home) are covered anywhere in the world only as optional cover for taking items away from home – you must have the away-from-home option selected for personal possessions/specifed items to be covered on holiday [4, 2, 1].  
- Admiral level: personal possessions and specified items away-from-home cover is optional — Admiral does not include worldwide personal possessions cover by default; you must have chosen the optional cover for items to be covered on holiday [1, 2].

Material conditions and limits that apply
- Any cover is subject to the personal possessions limit or specified item amounts shown on your Home Policy Schedule and the policy exclusions (for example items must be in your possession or in a secure place, limits for theft from vehicles, and exclusions such as loss after property has been outside the UK for more than 90 days) [2, 1].
