# Question

Is my phone covered when away from home?

# Answer

Yes — your phone can be covered away from home, but only if it’s included under the policy option you’ve chosen and any specified-item rules are met.

- Personal possessions cover:
  - Platinum policies include personal possessions anywhere in the world, so a phone classed as a personal possession would be covered away from home under Platinum [6].  
  - For Gold and Admiral, personal possessions are optional; you are only covered away from home if you have chosen that optional personal possessions cover on your Home Policy Schedule [2, 9]. Gold’s wording also confirms optional cover can extend worldwide when you’ve asked to cover personal possessions away from home [4].

- Specified items (if you listed the phone as a specified item):
  - Specified items are covered away from home up to the amount shown for each item on your Home Policy Schedule, but only if “Cover away from your home” is shown as “yes” for that item [1]. Platinum, Gold and Admiral can all have specified-item cover as an optional choice per the table [1].

- Important limits and conditions that affect cover:
  - High-risk or unspecified-item limits may apply (e.g. items worth more than the ‘Unspecified item limit’ aren’t covered under the personal possessions section) and loss while the item is not with you or your family (for example not being worn/carried or not within reach) is excluded under personal possessions/specifed items [2, 1].  
  - Theft from a motor vehicle has additional requirements and a £1,000 maximum for such thefts [2, 8].  
  - If you have specified the phone as a specified item and opted to cover it away from home, you may instead have worldwide cover for that item regardless of policy level, provided “Cover away from your home” is yes on the schedule [3, 1].

If you want a definitive answer for your situation, check your Home Policy Schedule to see whether personal possessions cover is included (or the phone is listed as a specified item with “Cover away from your home” set to yes) and the individual limit shown for the phone [9, 1].
