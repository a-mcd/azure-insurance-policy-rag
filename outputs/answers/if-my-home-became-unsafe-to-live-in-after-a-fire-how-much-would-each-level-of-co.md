# Question

If my home became unsafe to live in after a fire, how much would each level of cover pay for somewhere else for my family and pets to stay?

# Answer

Admiral: Up to £50,000 for temporary accommodation for your family and pets if your home is not safe to live in following an insured event [5].

Gold: Up to £75,000 for temporary accommodation for your family and pets if your home is not safe to live in following an insured event [4].

Platinum: Up to £100,000 for temporary accommodation for your family and pets if your home is not safe to live in following an insured event [6].

In addition, if you have Home Emergency cover (standard on Gold) it provides up to £500 (maximum £250 per night) for alternative accommodation and transport if your home remains uninhabitable overnight [2, 9]. If you have Home Emergency Extra cover (standard on Platinum) it provides up to £1,000 (maximum £250 per night) for alternative accommodation and transport [7, 9].
