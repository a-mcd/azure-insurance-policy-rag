# Question

Is my phone covered when away from home?

# Answer

Short answer: It depends on your policy level and whether you’ve chosen the optional cover — a phone can be covered away from home as a personal possession or a specified item, subject to limits and conditions.

Explanation:
- On Platinum, personal possessions (which would include a phone) are included and “We cover personal possessions anywhere in the world.” which means a phone is covered away from home under Platinum [6, 2].  
- On Gold and Admiral, personal possessions and specified items are optional; you’re only covered away from home if you have chosen that optional cover and your Home Policy Schedule shows “Cover away from your home” as yes for the item or you have selected personal possessions cover away from home [9, 2, 1].  
- Specified items listed on your Home Policy Schedule are covered away from home up to the individual amount shown, but only if “Cover away from your home” is shown as ‘yes’ for those items [1].  
- Theft from a motor vehicle is limited to £1,000 and requires that windows, doors and boot were closed and locked and the items hidden from view; other general exclusions (for example items not with you, things kept in storage, items abroad for more than 90 days) also apply [1, 2, 8].  

If you want a definitive yes/no for your phone, check your Home Policy Schedule to see whether personal possessions or the phone as a specified item is insured away from home and the limits that apply. [2, 1, 9]
