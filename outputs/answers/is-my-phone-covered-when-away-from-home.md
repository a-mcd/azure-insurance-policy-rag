# Question

Is my phone covered when away from home?

# Answer

Yes — your phone can be covered away from home, but it depends which part of cover you have and any optional choices.

- If your phone is insured as a specified item that is shown on your Home Policy Schedule with “Cover away from your home” set to yes, that specified item is covered away from home (Admiral, Gold and Platinum treat specified items as optional) [1].  
- If your phone is insured as part of “Personal possessions”: on Platinum it is included and the policy says personal possessions are covered anywhere in the world; on Gold and Admiral personal possessions are optional and only cover away from home applies if you have chosen that optional cover (Gold states optional cover for personal possessions; Admiral lists personal possessions optional) — Platinum: “We cover personal possessions anywhere in the world” [6, 9, 2, 7].  
- Exclusions that materially affect cover include loss or damage to items not with you or your family at the time (for example not being worn, carried or within reach), high‑risk items above the unspecified item limit, items outside the UK/Isle of Man/Channel Islands for more than 90 days, loss/damage while being used (for remote‑controlled models/sporting equipment) and limits on theft from a motor vehicle (maximum £1,000) and theft from vehicles only where windows/doors/boot were closed/locked and items hidden — these exclusions apply to personal possessions and specified items as shown [1, 2, 8].  

If you want a definitive answer for your phone, check your Home Policy Schedule to see whether the phone is listed as a specified item with “Cover away from your home” = yes, or whether you have personal possessions cover (and which policy level you hold) — those Schedule entries determine whether cover applies [1, 9].
