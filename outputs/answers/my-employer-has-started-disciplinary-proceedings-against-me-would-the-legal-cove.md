# Question

My employer has started disciplinary proceedings against me. Would the legal cover help with the dispute, or is this excluded?

# Answer

Direct answer: It may be covered only in limited circumstances — disciplinary proceedings while you are still employed are excluded for Employment disputes but legal defence cover can apply if the proceedings are a formal disciplinary hearing brought by a professional or regulatory body or arise from a criminal or covered civil investigation connected to your work. [2, 1, 4, 5]

Explanation and material conditions:
- Employment disputes cover a dispute relating to your contract of employment, but “a dispute is deemed to have occurred once all employer’s disciplinary hearings and internal grievance procedures have been completed,” and the policy excludes “employment claims relating to an employer’s disciplinary and internal grievance procedures or settlement agreements while you’re still employed.” This means routine disciplinary or internal grievance procedures started by your employer while you remain employed are excluded under the Employment disputes insured incident. [2, 1, 3]

- Separately, the Legal defence insured incident can cover the costs to defend you if “a formal investigation or disciplinary hearing is brought against you by a professional or regulatory body” or if you are contacted by police/prosecuted/subject to certain civil actions arising from your work as an employee (for example under discrimination or data protection legislation). If your employer’s disciplinary proceedings are of that professional/regulatory or criminal/certain-civil character, Legal defence may apply. [4, 5]

- Exclusions and conditions that may affect cover include: specific exclusions within Legal defence (for example claims involving assault, violence, sexual misconduct, alcohol or illegal drugs, cyber‑attacks, HMRC criminal enquiries, or driving a motor vehicle) and the policy’s requirements about instructing the appointed representative and not settling/withdrawing a claim without the insurer’s agreement. Whether Legal defence applies depends on the nature and source of the disciplinary proceedings. [5, 9]

If different facts would produce a different outcome: If the disciplinary action is a standard internal employer disciplinary procedure while you remain employed, it is excluded under Employment disputes. If the action is a formal disciplinary hearing by a professional or regulatory body or a criminal/civil investigation as described under Legal defence, that Legal defence cover may apply. [1, 2, 4, 5]
