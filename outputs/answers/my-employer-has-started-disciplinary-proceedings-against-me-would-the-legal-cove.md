# Question

My employer has started disciplinary proceedings against me. Would the legal cover help with the dispute, or is this excluded?

# Answer

Direct answer: The legal cover will not help while your employer’s disciplinary proceedings are still ongoing — such employment disciplinary and internal grievance matters are excluded until the disciplinary process is completed [1, 2].

Explanation:
- The policy’s Employment disputes section covers disputes relating to your employment contract but states that “a dispute is deemed to have occurred once all employer’s disciplinary hearings and internal grievance procedures have been completed” [2, 3].  
- The Are there any restrictions on cover? wording explicitly lists “Employment claims relating to an employer’s disciplinary and internal grievance procedures or settlement agreements while you’re still employed” as restricted (excluded) cover [1].  
- Separately, the Legal defence section does provide cover for a formal disciplinary hearing brought by a professional or regulatory body and for criminal/prosecution or certain civil actions arising from your work; that is a different insured event and would apply only if those specific circumstances exist (for example a professional/regulatory disciplinary hearing) — but the excerpts do not show cover for an employer’s internal disciplinary procedure while it is ongoing [4, 5].

If different facts would change the outcome:
- If the matter becomes an employment “dispute” after all employer disciplinary hearings and internal grievance procedures have been completed, the Employment disputes cover may then apply [2, 3]. [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]
