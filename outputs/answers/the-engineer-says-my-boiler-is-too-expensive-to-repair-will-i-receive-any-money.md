# Question

The engineer says my boiler is too expensive to repair. Will I receive any money towards replacing it?

# Answer

Yes — if you have Home Emergency Extra (Platinum) cover, the policy provides up to £250 towards a replacement boiler declared beyond economic repair; this is not included on Home Emergency (Gold) cover. [5, 1]

Explanation and key conditions:
- Beyond economic repair is defined as the repair cost being more than replacing it, and the £250 payment applies under Home Emergency Extra cover (Platinum) only; Home Emergency cover (Gold) does not include this benefit. [5]
- The £250 is a contribution “towards the cost of a new boiler” (not a guaranteed full replacement) and is stated as the amount the policy will pay. [1, 4]
- The boiler must be eligible under the boiler and heating system section (for example, boilers outside the home are excluded unless they provide the main heating; underfloor or commercial boilers over 70kW/hr are not covered). If your boiler falls into any of those exclusions, the beyond-economic-repair payment would not apply. [3, 7]
