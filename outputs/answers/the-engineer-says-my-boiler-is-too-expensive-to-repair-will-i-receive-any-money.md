# Question

The engineer says my boiler is too expensive to repair. Will I receive any money towards replacing it?

# Answer

Yes — but only if you have Home Emergency Extra cover.

Under Home Emergency Extra cover the policy provides up to £250 towards the cost of a replacement boiler if your heating system is declared beyond economic repair (meaning repair would cost more than replacement) [5]. This same amount is also stated in the Home Emergency Extra cover wording: “we'll pay you £250 towards the cost of a replacement” when beyond economical repair [1] and in the reimbursement table as “£250 (including VAT) towards the cost of a new boiler” under Home Emergency Extra cover [4].

If you only have the standard Home Emergency cover (not the Extra option), cover for a boiler beyond economic repair is not included [5]. Also the usual exclusions for boilers (for example boilers outside the home unless they are the main source of heating, and commercial or >70kW/hr boilers) still apply and would affect eligibility [7, 3].
