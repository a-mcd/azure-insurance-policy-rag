# Question

I have the gold policy, is my phone covered if I take it on holiday?

# Answer

Yes — your phone can be covered on holiday if it is included under the policy’s Personal possessions optional cover. The Gold policy’s Optional Cover states personal possessions you have asked to cover away from home are covered anywhere in the world, and you can increase that personal possessions cover up to a maximum of £20,000 [1, 3].
