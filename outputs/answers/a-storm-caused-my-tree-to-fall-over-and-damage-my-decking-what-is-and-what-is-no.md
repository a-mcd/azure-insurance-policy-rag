# Question

A storm caused my tree to fall over and damage my decking. What is and what is not covered?

# Answer

Short answer: Your buildings cover may pay to repair decking damaged by a fallen tree from the storm, but damage to gates, fences or hedges is excluded and removal costs have limits — and the storm must meet the policy’s storm definition. [3, 5, 1]

Explanation and key points:
- Storm definition requirement: The insurer only covers storm damage if the weather meets its defined storm conditions (for example wind gusts of at least 55mph, heavy rain at least 25mm/hr, snow 30cm/24h, or damaging hail). They use independent weather reports to check this. If the weather does not meet that definition, there is no cover for wind/rain/snow/sleet unless it was a storm as defined. [1, 7]

- Falling trees and branches (buildings): The buildings section includes cover for damage caused by falling trees and branches, so damage to your decking (part of buildings cover if decking is insured as buildings) from a tree falling in a storm is covered under that event, subject to the storm definition and any applicable excesses or limits shown in your Home Policy Schedule. [5, 3]

- Exclusions and removal costs: The policy does not cover loss or damage to gates, fences or hedges from storm or from falling trees/branches. The cost of removing fallen trees or branches is only covered where they have caused damage to the buildings; the cost of removing a tree that hasn’t caused building damage, the cost of removing parts remaining below ground, and the cost of restoring the site are excluded. [2, 5, 3]

What you should check on your policy schedule or when you claim:
- Whether decking is insured under your buildings sum insured (the excerpts only show that falling trees are covered under the buildings policy; they do not confirm your specific schedule entries). If decking is treated as part of the buildings, the falling-tree cover applies; if it is not insured as buildings, the excerpts do not establish cover. [5]

- The applicable excess for storm or flood shown in your Home Policy Schedule. [3]

If any of these facts (for example whether the decking is included as buildings) are different, that may change whether the buildings falling-tree cover applies; the excerpts do not provide your schedule details so this cannot be established here. [5]
