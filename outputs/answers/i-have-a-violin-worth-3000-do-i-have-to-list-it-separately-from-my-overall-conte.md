# Question

I have a violin worth £3000. Do I have to list it separately from my overall contents?

# Answer

Yes — you must list it as a specified item because a violin is a “musical instrument” (a high‑risk item) and specified items are high‑risk items worth more than £2,000, which must be listed in your Home Policy Schedule [1, 8, 4].
