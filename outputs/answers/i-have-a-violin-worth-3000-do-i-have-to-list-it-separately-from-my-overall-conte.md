# Question

I have a violin worth £3000. Do I have to list it separately from my overall contents?

# Answer

Yes — you must list the violin as a specified item if it is worth more than £2,000. Specified items are high‑risk items (which include musical instruments) worth over £2,000 and “must be listed in your Home Policy Schedule” [8, 1, 4]. If you do not list it, the policy will not pay more than the Unspecified item limit for it (and all high‑risk items over that limit must be specified) [4, 7]. Also note whether specifying is optional or included depends on level: personal possessions cover for high‑risk items is optional on Admiral and Gold but included on Platinum; however the requirement to list items over £2,000 as specified still applies [10, 4, 6].
