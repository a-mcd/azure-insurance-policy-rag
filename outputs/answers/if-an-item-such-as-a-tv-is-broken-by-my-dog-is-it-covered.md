# Question

If an item such as a TV is broken by my dog, is it covered?

# Answer

Direct answer: No — damage to a TV caused by your dog is not covered.

Explanation: Accidental damage to audio‑visual equipment (including televisions) is covered under the Contents accidental damage section for Gold and Platinum (and optionally for Admiral) [4]. However, the policy specifically excludes loss or damage caused by pets through chewing, scratching, tearing, fouling or vomiting for Buildings and Contents (all levels) and in the accidental damage exclusions for contents and buildings [1, 3, 5, 6]. Therefore damage to a TV caused by your dog falls within that pets exclusion and isn’t covered [3].
