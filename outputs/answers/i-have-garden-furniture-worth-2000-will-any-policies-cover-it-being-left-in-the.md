# Question

I have garden furniture worth £2000 will any policies cover it being left in the garden?

# Answer

No — under the supplied excerpts garden furniture left in the garden would not be fully covered for the £2,000 value unless you have the higher-level/optional garden contents or personal possessions cover that applies.

Explanation:
- Gold: Garden contents cover is up to £1,000 for items kept in your garden such as patio furniture, so this would provide cover up to £1,000 only (not the full £2,000) [2]. Theft from garages/outbuildings is separate and up to £5,000 but does not change the garden contents limit shown for garden items [8, 2].
- Platinum: Garden contents cover is up to £2,500 for items kept in your garden such as patio furniture, so Platinum would provide cover up to £2,500 and thus could cover the £2,000 (within the stated limit) [4].
- Admiral: Only garden plants limit (£1,000) is shown; no garden contents limit for patio furniture is provided in the Admiral excerpts, so cover for garden furniture left in the garden cannot be established from the Admiral excerpts supplied [5, 6].

Additional relevant exclusions and options:
- Personal possessions cover excludes items lost or damaged in your home or on the land your home stands on unless the policy level/option specifically includes cover away from home; personal possessions is optional at Gold and Admiral and included at Platinum — but the personal possessions section expressly excludes items lost or damaged on the land that your home stands on [9]. Specified items can be covered away from home only if ‘Cover away from your home’ is shown as ‘yes’ on the schedule; plants are excluded from specified items [7].
- Therefore, whether the £2,000 garden furniture is covered depends on the policy level: Platinum garden contents provides cover up to £2,500 (so would cover £2,000 within that limit) [4]; Gold garden contents provides up to £1,000 (so would not fully cover £2,000) [2]; Admiral garden contents cover for patio furniture is not shown in the supplied excerpts so cannot be established from these documents [5, 6].
