# Question

I have garden furniture worth £2000 will any policies cover it being left in the garden?

# Answer

Short answer: Yes — Platinum and Gold policies provide some cover for garden furniture left in the garden, but limits apply and Admiral provides less cover for garden items. [4, 2, 6]

Explanation:
- Platinum level: Garden contents (including patio furniture) are covered up to £2,500 for items kept in your garden such as patio furniture [4]. The Platinum personal possessions option is included for away-from-home cover but that does not affect garden contents on the land your home stands on [9, 4].
- Gold level: Garden contents (including patio furniture) are covered up to £1,000 for items kept in your garden such as patio furniture [2]. Gold also shows garden plants separately (not relevant to furniture) [1, 6]. Gold’s Personal Possessions cover is optional and relates to items away from home, not garden contents, but you may increase personal possessions limits if selected [10, 7].
- Admiral level: The extracts show garden plants cover up to £1,000 but do not show a garden contents amount for patio furniture; therefore from the supplied excerpts Admiral’s garden-furniture limit cannot be established here and cannot be relied on for cover of a £2,000 set [5, 6].

What this means for your £2,000 garden furniture:
- Under Platinum, the furniture “is within the stated limit” only up to £2,500, so Platinum could provide cover up to that amount for patio furniture kept in your garden [4].
- Under Gold, cover is “up to” £1,000, so Gold would not fully cover a £2,000 loss for garden furniture; it provides cover up to £1,000 [2].
- From the supplied excerpts, Admiral’s cover for garden furniture is not shown, so whether Admiral would cover the £2,000 is not determinable from these documents alone [5, 6].

Material conditions and exclusions that may affect a claim (from the excerpts): garden plants have exclusions (loss caused by you/family, storm or flood, oil escaping) which apply to that section; garden contents covers patio furniture as stated but other general exclusions or requirements for theft or damage are not shown for garden contents in the supplied snippets, so further policy terms could apply but are not provided here [6, 2, 7].
