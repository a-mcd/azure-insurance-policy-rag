# Question

If someone broke into my detached garage and stole my tools. Which levels of home insurance would cover them, and how much could I claim?

# Answer

Admiral would not cover theft from your detached garage; Gold and Platinum would cover it — Gold provides cover up to £5,000 and Platinum provides cover up to £7,500. [3, 1, 2]

Explanation:
- The Guide’s coverage table shows “Theft from garages and outbuildings” is not included for Admiral but is included for Gold and Platinum. [3]
- The Gold Home Insurance policy states Theft from Garages and Outbuildings is covered up to £5,000. [1]
- The Platinum Home Insurance policy states Theft from Garages and Outbuildings is covered up to £7,500. [2]

Material conditions and exclusions that may affect a claim:
- The guide excludes money, credit cards and high‑risk items from this cover, and excludes theft from greenhouses. [3]
- The Events table notes loss or damage to contents in outbuildings may instead be covered under “Theft from garages and outbuildings” (i.e. the Gold/Platinum cover) and lists general exclusions such as loss caused by you, your family or anyone invited into your home, loss while your home is unoccupied, and other conditions; these could affect a claim depending on the circumstances. [5]

I cannot determine from the excerpts whether your stolen tools are classed as covered contents (for example, whether they are “high‑risk items”) or whether any specific claim conditions (such as the garage being unlocked or the home being unoccupied) apply; those facts would change whether a claim is payable. [3, 5]
