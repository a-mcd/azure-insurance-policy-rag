# Question

If someone broke into my detached garage and stole my tools. Which levels of home insurance would cover them, and how much could I claim?

# Answer

Admiral: Not covered. Gold: Covered up to £5,000. Platinum: Covered up to £7,500.

Explanation:
- Admiral level does not include theft from garages and outbuildings [3, 7].  
- Gold includes theft from garages and outbuildings and provides cover up to £5,000 for theft from garages and outbuildings [3, 1].  
- Platinum includes theft from garages and outbuildings and provides cover up to £7,500 for theft from garages and outbuildings [3, 2].  

Conditions and exclusions that may affect a claim:
- The theft cover applies to contents kept locked in your outbuildings on the land your home stands on; money, credit cards and high‑risk items, and theft from greenhouses are not covered under this section [3].  
- The general contents theft section excludes contents in outbuildings unless covered under the “Theft from garages and outbuildings” (Gold/Platinum) or Garden Contents; other general exclusions include theft caused by you or invited persons, theft while your home is unoccupied, and other listed exclusions [5, 3].  
- How much would actually be paid (for example whether items are replaced or cash paid) is subject to the policy’s claims settlement terms on contents (replace, repair or pay) [8].
