# Question

I have a bike worth £5000 which I travel with. How can I ensure it?

# Answer

You need to add it as a specified bicycle under the optional “Bikes over £350” cover so it’s insured for travel. [5]

Explanation:
- Unspecified cover only insures bicycles up to the Unspecified cycle limit (typically £350 worldwide) so a £5,000 bike exceeds that and would be classed as a specified bicycle and not fully covered as an unspecified bicycle. [3, 7]
- The policy offers optional cover for bikes over £350 allowing bikes up to £10,000 per bike (up to a £20,000 total) when you add this optional cover — you must add that optional cover to insure a £5,000 bicycle. [5, 8] 
- Specified bicycles shown in your Home Policy Schedule can be covered anywhere in the world if the ‘Cover away from your home’ box for that bicycle is set to ‘Yes’ in the Bicycles table. You must list the bicycle in your Home Policy Schedule and select cover away from home if you intend to travel with it. [2, 7]
- Accessories are covered up to 10% of the specified bicycle value. [2]

If you want confirmation of the exact Unspecified cycle limit, any extra security requirements, and to ensure ‘Cover away from your home’ is selected for your bike, check your Home Policy Schedule (these details determine the applicable limits and conditions). [3, 7]
