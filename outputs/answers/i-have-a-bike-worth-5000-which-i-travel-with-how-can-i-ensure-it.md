# Question

I have a bike worth £5000 which I travel with. How can I ensure it?

# Answer

You must add it as a specified bicycle (optional cover) to ensure it is covered away from home.

Explanation:
- The policies only automatically cover pedal cycles up to £350 anywhere in the world under Contents cover; your £5,000 bike exceeds that automatic worldwide unspecified limit [1, 4, 6]. [1]
- Bikes worth more than the unspecified limit must be listed as specified bicycles in your Home Policy Schedule to be fully covered; specified bicycle cover (including cover away from your home) is optional and depends on the ‘Bicycles’ table and the ‘Cover away from your home’ selection in your schedule [7, 2]. [7]
- The policies allow adding bikes over £350 up to £10,000 per bike (subject to a maximum total) when you add them as specified bikes [5, 8, 9]. [5]
- Specified bicycles show in the Bicycles table and only those with ‘Cover away from your home’ set to ‘Yes’ are covered anywhere in the world; accessories are covered up to 10% of the specified bicycle value [2]. [2]
- The Platinum policy also expressly states that specified bikes you have asked to cover away from home will be covered anywhere in the world [10]. [10]

Practical next steps (based only on the excerpts):
- Ask your insurer to add the £5,000 bicycle as a specified bicycle in your Home Policy Schedule and ensure ‘Cover away from your home’ is set to ‘Yes’ so it is covered while you travel [7, 2, 10]. [7]
