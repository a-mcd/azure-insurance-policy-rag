# Question

I have a bike worth £200 which I travel with. How can I ensure it?

# Answer

Yes — your £200 bike can be insured as an unspecified bicycle under Contents cover.

Explanation:
- Unspecified bicycles cover any bicycle anywhere in the world up to the “Unspecified cycle limit”, and you can find that limit in your Home Policy Schedule [3].
- All levels (Admiral, Gold, Platinum) include unspecified bicycle cover (not optional) subject to that unspecified limit [3].
- The policies state pedal cycles are covered up to £350 anywhere in the world if Contents cover is purchased, which shows that a £200 bike falls within that commonly stated per-cycle amount [6, 2, 4].
- This section also pays up to 10% of the unspecified bicycle limit for accessories attached to the bicycle [3].
- You must have Contents insurance to be covered and a compulsory contents excess (and any voluntary excess) applies to any claim [8].

If you want cover for more than the unspecified limit (for example if your bike were worth more than the unspecified limit or over £350), you would need to list it as a specified bicycle in your Home Policy Schedule or add optional higher-value bike cover; but that does not apply to a £200 bike under the evidence shown here [5, 7, 9, 10].
