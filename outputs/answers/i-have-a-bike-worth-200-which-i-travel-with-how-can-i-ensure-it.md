# Question

I have a bike worth £200 which I travel with. How can I ensure it?

# Answer

Yes — your £200 bike can be covered as an unspecified bicycle under any level (Admiral, Gold or Platinum) if you have Contents Cover, because unspecified cycles are covered anywhere in the world up to the unspecified bicycle limit and pedal cycles are covered up to £350 when Contents Cover is purchased [3, 6]. 

Requirements and limits that materially affect this:
- The policy covers pedal cycles worth up to £350 anywhere in the world if Contents Cover is purchased, so a £200 bike is within that amount (Admiral, Gold and Platinum) [6, 2, 4]. 
- Unspecified bicycle cover also pays up to 10% of the unspecified bicycle limit for accessories attached to the bicycle [3]. 
- Theft when the bike is left unattended is not covered unless it is locked to an immovable object, kept in a locked and secure building only you and your family can access, or kept inside/locked to a vehicle using a cycle rack — those conditions apply to unspecified bicycles [3]. 

If your bike were worth more than the unspecified bicycle limit it would need to be listed as a specified bicycle in your Home Policy Schedule (and you’d need to select “Cover away from your home” if you want it covered away from home) [5, 1].
