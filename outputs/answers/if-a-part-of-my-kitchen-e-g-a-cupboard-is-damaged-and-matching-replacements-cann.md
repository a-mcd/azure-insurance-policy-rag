# Question

If a part of my kitchen e.g. a cupboard is damaged and matching replacements cannot be found will all the matching cupboards be replaced?

# Answer

Short answer: Only on the Platinum policy — yes, otherwise no.

Explanation:
- The Platinum policy includes matching items cover that says if one item in a matching set (for example a fitted kitchen unit) is damaged and the insurer cannot replace that individual item they will replace the whole set [4].  
- The Coverage table confirms that “Matching items” (repairing or replacing the whole set or suite when part is damaged beyond repair and a replacement cannot be found) is included for Platinum but is not included for Admiral or Gold [6].  
- For Admiral/Gold the policy treats an individual item from a matching set as a single item and will only pay for the items actually lost or damaged, not the undamaged pieces of the set [5] and [6].  
- The insurer may decide whether to repair, replace or pay and may take ownership of damaged property; they class each individual item from a matching set as a single item unless Matching items is shown in your Home Policy Schedule [1].
