# Question

While moving some furniture, I accidentally cracked the fixed glass in my patio door. Would my insurance pay for the repair?

# Answer

Yes — your policy can cover repair of the cracked fixed glass in your patio door, provided your level of cover includes accidental breakage of fitted glass and the breakage meets the policy definition of accidental breakage.

Explanation:
- The policy covers accidental breakage of fitted glass in doors under “Accidental breakage of glass and sanitary fixtures” (Admiral: optional; Gold: included; Platinum: included) [1].  
- Accidental breakage is defined as sudden, unexpected and visible breakage caused by a specific external force (so the incident must meet that definition) [7].  
- Gold policy wording also confirms accidental breakage of glass and sanitary ware is insured (Gold wording) [2].  
- If you have Admiral level you must have chosen the optional accidental breakage cover; Gold and Platinum include it automatically [1].  
- The Home Emergency sections only cover securing broken external windows/doors in an emergency and exclude internal windows that do not affect security; they do not replace the buildings/accidental-breakage cover for repairing fitted glass [4, 5].  

What affects the outcome:
- You’ll need to pay any accidental-damage/breakage excess shown on your Home Policy Schedule and claims may be refused if the breakage is excluded under the listed exclusions (for example if it occurred while the home was unoccupied or was caused by an excluded party or pest) [8].  

If you want a definitive entitlement, confirm your policy level (Admiral with the optional cover, Gold, or Platinum) and the excess on your Home Policy Schedule, and that the incident meets the policy definition of accidental breakage [1, 7, 8].
