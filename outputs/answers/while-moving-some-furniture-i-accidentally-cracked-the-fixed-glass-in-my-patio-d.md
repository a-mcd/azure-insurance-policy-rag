# Question

While moving some furniture, I accidentally cracked the fixed glass in my patio door. Would my insurance pay for the repair?

# Answer

Yes — your policy can cover the repair of the fixed glass in the patio door as accidental breakage of fitted glass under buildings cover, provided your policy level includes that cover and the breakage meets the policy definition of accidental breakage.

Explanation:
- The buildings accidental breakage section covers accidental breakage of fitted glass in doors (including fixed glass in doors) [1]. 
- The policy defines accidental breakage as sudden, unexpected and visible breakage caused by a specific external force, which would apply to accidentally cracking the glass while moving furniture if that description matches the incident [7]. 
- Whether cover applies depends on your policy level: Gold and Platinum include this cover; Admiral shows it as optional (so it only applies if you chose the optional cover) [1]. Cite: [1]. 
- You must also meet any excess shown in your Home Policy Schedule and the claim must not be excluded by the listed exclusions (for example the property being unoccupied or damage caused by excluded pests or by a tenant/paying guest) [8]. Cite: [8].

If you would like a definitive answer for your policy, tell me which policy level (Admiral, Gold or Platinum) is on your Home Policy Schedule and confirm the circumstances match the accidental breakage definition.
