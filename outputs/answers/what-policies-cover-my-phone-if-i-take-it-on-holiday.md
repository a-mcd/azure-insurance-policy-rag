# Question

What policies cover my phone if I take it on holiday?

# Answer

Yes — Platinum cover includes your phone anywhere in the world as personal possessions; Gold and Admiral only cover it away from home if you have taken the optional “cover away from your home” for personal possessions or specified items.

Explanation:
- Platinum: personal possessions are covered anywhere in the world (so a phone taken on holiday is covered under personal possessions) [5]. Also Platinum’s personal possessions is shown as included for away-from-home cover in the Coverage by level table (Personal possessions: Platinum: Included) [2].
- Gold: personal possessions away from home are optional and only covered worldwide if you have taken the optional cover for personal possessions/ specified items to cover them away from home (Gold’s Where am I covered says optional cover for personal possessions/specifed items you have asked to cover away from home will cover you anywhere in the world) [4]; the Coverage by level table shows Gold: Optional for Personal possessions and Specified items away from home [2, 1].
- Admiral: Personal possessions and Specified items away from home are optional (Admiral: Optional) so a phone is only covered away from home if you have bought that optional cover [2, 1].

Material conditions and exclusions that affect cover when you take a phone on holiday:
- The phone must be with you or your family at the time of loss; items not with you or out of reach are not covered under personal possessions/specified items away from home [2, 1].
- Loss or damage to property that has been outside the UK, Isle of Man and Channel Islands for more than 90 days in a row is excluded (unless on an operational tour) [2, 1].
- Theft from a motor vehicle has specific requirements and a maximum payable of £1,000 for any theft from a motor vehicle [2, 1].
- Other general policy exclusions (for example war/terrorism, riot outside UK/IoM/CI, radioactivity, gradual damage, electronic data/computer virus etc.) also apply where relevant [7].

If you want a definitive answer for your policy you need to confirm which level (Admiral, Gold or Platinum) applies to your policy and whether you purchased the optional “cover away from your home” for personal possessions or specified items (for Admiral and Gold) — that fact is not shown in the provided excerpts.
