# Question

What policies cover my phone if I take it on holiday?

# Answer

Personal possessions cover (Gold and Platinum) or Specified items cover (Admiral, Gold, Platinum as optional) will cover a phone taken on holiday, subject to the limits and conditions below.

Explanation and applicable policy points:
1. Platinum automatically covers personal possessions anywhere in the world, so a phone taken on holiday is covered under Personal possessions if it is insured as such on Platinum [5].  
2. Gold provides cover to personal possessions away from home if you have chosen the optional “cover away from home” for personal possessions; the policy says optional cover: for personal possessions you have asked to cover away from home, we will cover you anywhere in the world [4]. However the standard Gold table marks Personal possessions as Optional for away-from-home cover, so you must have that option selected on your schedule [2].  
3. Admiral, Gold and Platinum all allow Specified items to be covered away from home only if “Cover away from your home” for that specified item is shown as ‘yes’ on your Home Policy Schedule; Specified items is Optional for Admiral and Gold and Optional for Platinum in the table [1].  
4. General limits and conditions that apply to both Personal possessions and Specified items include exclusions such as items not with you or your family at the time of loss, loss occurring after property has been outside the UK/Isle of Man/Channel Islands for more than 90 days in a row (unless an operational tour), theft from an unattended vehicle subject to vehicle-security and a maximum £1,000 for theft from a motor vehicle, and requirements to have items in your possession or left in a secure place (for example locked in your room or safe) for theft claims [1, 2].  
5. For contents taken temporarily away from home there is separate cover for Contents temporarily away from home (Gold and Platinum included, Admiral not included) but this applies to contents kept in a family member’s home or a purpose-built storage facility anywhere in the world for up to 90 days and excludes loss while being transported or to contents not previously kept in your home—this does not directly extend cover for a phone you carry on holiday unless those exact conditions apply [6].  
6. The Platinum policy also states that for specified items you have asked to cover away from home, and for specified bikes, the insurer will cover you anywhere in the world (confirming worldwide cover if the specified-item away-from-home option is selected) [3].

What cannot be established from the excerpts:
- The exact monetary limits applicable to your phone (personal possessions limit or individual specified-item amount) cannot be determined from the excerpts; you must check your Home Policy Schedule for the specific limits and whether “cover away from your home” is shown as ‘yes’ for any specified item [1, 2].
