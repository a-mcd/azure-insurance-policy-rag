# Question

I am leaving my house empty for ten weeks while travelling. Would this affect what I can claim for while I am away?

# Answer

Yes — leaving your home empty for ten weeks would affect cover unless you’ve agreed otherwise with the insurer.

Explanation:
- The policies for Admiral, Gold and Platinum each give an unoccupancy allowance of up to 60 days before restrictions apply [2, 4, 7].  
- All three policy levels state that loss or damage under many sections is excluded if the home is left unoccupied for more than 60 days (empty homes exclusion) [3, 6, 8].  
- The general definition of “Unoccupied” says the home is classed as unoccupied if it’s not lived in by you or your family for more than your ‘days unoccupied limit’ shown in your Home Policy Schedule and that you must tell the insurer beforehand if the home will be unoccupied for longer than that limit so you aren’t left without cover [1].

Because ten weeks is longer than 60 days, these excerpts show cover could be restricted or excluded for many sections unless you have agreed otherwise with the insurer [1, 2, 3, 4, 7, 8].
